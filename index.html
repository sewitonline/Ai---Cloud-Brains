Common Questions

You do. Your AI brain runs on a private server set up for your business, and you control who has access.

No. We build it, connect your apps and train you and your team. You use it by asking questions and reviewing what the agents do.

We plug your brain into them, so you keep working the way you do today. Everything flows into your custom dashboard and mobile app. Some apps you may be able to cancel once the brain does that job, and the audit shows you which.

Will AI replace my team?

It takes the repeat work off their plate so they can focus on customers and growth. If you run solo, it covers the work you'd otherwise have to hire for.
